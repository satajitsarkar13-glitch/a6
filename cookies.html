Digital Privacy

Cookie & Tracking Technology Policy

Transparent disclosure of digital cookies, storage mechanisms, and telemetry preferences.

1. Understanding Cookies & Digital Tokens

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device when you navigate digital websites. At Sable & Crest, we respect your digital sanctuary just as we respect your personal privacy. We utilize cookies strictly to ensure seamless platform navigation, preserve your bespoke configurator preferences during active sessions, and analyze anonymized aggregate traffic performance.

2. Classification of Cookies Utilized

Our website utilizes the following categories of digital cookies and local storage tokens:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for fundamental website navigation, load balancing, security verification, and form submission integrity. These cookies do not store personally identifiable data and cannot be disabled in our systems.
  • Functional & Preference Cookies: Enable our bespoke 3D configurator studio to remember your selected leather hide, hardware metallurgy, and tote scale preferences while navigating between pages.
  • Analytical & Telemetry Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics (tracking tag: G-0LY0HY7L01) to gather anonymized telemetry regarding page visit frequencies, geographic regional trends, and device screen resolutions. All IP addresses are automatically anonymized prior to storage.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You maintain absolute sovereignty over cookie storage on your devices. You can configure your internet browser (e.g. Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge) to notify you whenever a cookie is set, block third-party cookies, or purge all stored cache data entirely. Please note that disabling strictly necessary functional cookies may limit the interactive performance of our bespoke tote configurator studio.

5. Policy Revisions & Inquiries

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect emerging digital regulatory standards. For questions regarding our cookie practices, please contact our technical privacy team at: privacy@sabletotecrest.com.
