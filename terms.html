Governing all bespoke commissions, digital interactions, and transactions with Sable & Crest.

If you do not consent to these terms in their entirety, you must discontinue use of this website immediately.

2. Nature of Bespoke Artisanal Goods

Every piece produced by Sable & Crest is an individually handcrafted work of leathercraft art. Due to the intrinsic organic characteristics of certified full-grain vegetable-tanned leather, slight natural variations in grain density, subtle growth marks, neck striations, and dye absorption nuances are natural hallmarks of authentic leather and do not constitute manufacturing defects.

3. Bespoke Commission & Production Timelines

Upon submitting a bespoke commission request and approving the technical specification dossier, a non-refundable deposit of fifty percent (50%) is required to initiate hide selection and bench production. Production schedules typically span four (4) to six (6) weeks, subject to rare leather hide availability and bench queue capacity. Final delivery is executed upon settlement of the remaining balance.

5. White-Glove Shipping, Risk of Loss & Customs

All shipments are dispatched via tracked, fully-insured courier services with mandatory adult signature verification upon receipt. Risk of loss passes to the client upon physical delivery. For cross-border deliveries outside the European Economic Area, clients are responsible for applicable local import tariffs, duties, and customs clearance charges assessed by their destination country.

6. Return, Exchange & Cancellation Policy

Standard catalog pieces in brand-new, unused, and pristine condition may be returned or exchanged within fourteen (14) calendar days of delivery, accompanied by original packaging, dust covers, and certificates of origin. Bespoke, custom-dimensioned, or monogrammed items are strictly non-refundable and exempt from standard return rights once bench production has commenced, as they are personalized to individual client specifications.

7. The Sable Lifetime Heirloom Warranty

We warrant that every Sable & Crest tote is free from structural defects in material craftsmanship, including seam stitching, structural bonding, handle rivet integrity, and bronze hardware casting. This warranty covers complimentary repair or replacement at our discretion for the natural lifespan of the product. The warranty excludes damage resulting from intentional misuse, unauthorized third-party modifications, chemical spills, or severe surface abrasion beyond normal patina evolution.
