Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last revised: January 2026 • Strict adherence to General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards.

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At Sable & Crest ("the Atelier", "we", "our", or "us"), safeguarding the confidentiality and integrity of your personal information is an inviolable cornerstone of our luxury client service. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, utilize, process, store, and protect information collected when you browse our website (sabletotecrest.com), commission bespoke leather goods, subscribe to our private communications, or engage with our atelier concierge.

2. Categories of Information Collected

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to deliver bespoke craftsmanship and exceptional white-glove client service:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full name, salutation, residential and delivery addresses, official telephone number, and email address provided during commission inquiries.
  • Bespoke Specification Records: Custom monogram initials, preferred leather grain selections, bespoke dimension parameters, and past craftsmanship commission history.
  • Transaction & Invoicing Information: Billing addresses, payment verification tokens, and VAT identification details processed through encrypted PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We never store raw credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, session duration, and referral sources collected via Google Analytics (G-0LY0HY7L01) for performance optimization.

3. Legal Grounds and Purpose of Data Processing

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases established under Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  1. Performance of Contract: To manufacture, customize, quality-inspect, insure, and deliver your bespoke leather tote bag to your designated address.
  2. Legitimate Interests: To maintain our permanent heirloom archive, verify warranty claims under the Sable Heirloom Warranty, and enhance site security.
  3. Legal Compliance: To satisfy statutory accounting, tax reporting, export compliance, and cross-border trade record-keeping requirements.
  4. Explicit Consent: To send you private invitations to limited batch leather allocations when you opt in to our atelier newsletter.

4. Data Retention and Archival Protocols

Because every Sable & Crest piece is covered by a lifetime craftsmanship warranty, we maintain permanent records of your tote's serial number, artisan bench mark, and original commission specifications in our encrypted Atelier Archive. This ensures that even decades later, our master craftsmen can accurately reference original leather dye lots and thread specifications during restoration. General transactional and marketing data is retained only for the duration permitted under European Union law.

5. Third-Party Disclosures & Sub-Processors

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to commercial advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances. Data is shared exclusively with vetted sub-processors essential to fulfilling your commission:

  • Specialized white-glove international insured couriers (e.g. DHL Express, FedEx Custom Critical).
  • Encrypted payment gateways conforming to Level 1 PCI-DSS standards.
  • Certified cloud infrastructure providers operating within the European Economic Area (EEA).

6. Your Statutory Rights as a Data Subject

Under international privacy regulations including GDPR, UK Data Protection Act, and CCPA, you possess the following rights:

  • The right to access and receive a copy of all personal data held about you.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • The right to request the erasure of your data ("Right to be Forgotten"), subject to mandatory legal tax retention requirements.
  • The right to restrict or object to specific automated processing activities.
  • The right to data portability in a structured, machine-readable format.

7. Data Protection Officer & Inquiries

To exercise any of your data rights or to submit a formal privacy inquiry, please contact our appointed Data Protection Officer at:
Email: privacy@sabletotecrest.com
Postal Address: DPO, Sable & Crest Atelier, 24 Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, 75008 Paris, France.
